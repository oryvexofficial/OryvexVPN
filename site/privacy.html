Privacy and trust

Know where your traffic goes.

Each route works differently. Your choices decide what is sent where.

This site

No accounts, passwords, payments or Cloudflare tokens. Links to GitHub leave this site.

Fox

An unofficial client for eligible Firefox Accounts. Mozilla handles account and service access under its own terms.

Your relay

If you deploy one, Fox may send account and service requests through it. Whoever runs the Cloudflare account can inspect them. General VPN traffic does not use it.

Tokens and settings

The Cloudflare API token is used for deployment and not saved by OryvexVPN. Fox keeps the relay address and secret locally. Keep both private.

Route differences

Tor, WireGuard, MASQUE, Fox, Bypass and MITM differ. Bypass and MITM are direct routes and do not change your public IP. MITM installs a local certificate.

Open source

Read the code, the Worker allowlist and the licence. No site can promise a network will stay reachable.
