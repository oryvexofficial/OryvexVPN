Install. Choose. Connect.

01 / INSTALL

Get the app

Install the Windows setup or the Android APK for your phone.

02 / CHOOSE

Pick your route

Use Auto, Tor, Fox or another connection from the Route section.

03 / CONNECT

Press connect

For Fox, first open Settings > Fox accounts and locations to sign in and choose a server.

Android can route the whole device through its VPN service. On Windows, Fox uses a local proxy; configure applications to use it.
