03 · Fox relay

Your own relay on Cloudflare.

If Mozilla services are hard to reach, deploy a small Worker from Fox settings. OryvexVPN uploads the bundled Worker, sets a random secret and saves the address into Fox.

  1. Deploy opens a short dialog in the app.
  2. Token Template opens Cloudflare with the right permissions pre-filled. Create the token and paste it.
  3. The app finds your account, uploads the Worker, enables workers.dev and sets the Fox relay URL.

The relay carries Fox account and service requests only, not general VPN traffic. It may help in Iran, but the sign-in web page itself, or workers.dev, can still be blocked on some networks. Use your own Cloudflare account: its owner can see relayed requests.

05

Short answers.

Is the Cloudflare relay a VPN?

No. It forwards Fox control-plane requests such as account and service calls. General traffic does not pass through it.

Where does my Cloudflare token go?

It is sent to Cloudflare to deploy the Worker and is not saved in OryvexVPN settings. Fox stores the relay URL and secret locally.

Does every route change my public IP?

No. Bypass and MITM are direct routes and do not change it. Check the route description in the app.

Is Fox an official Mozilla product?

No. Fox is an unofficial client and is not made, endorsed or supported by Mozilla.
